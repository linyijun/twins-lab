# Research Projects

## [SIGSPATIAL ’25] Fine-Scale Soil Mapping in Alaska with Multimodal Machine Learning
image: /research/soil.jpg
links: Paper|#, GitHub|#, arXiv|#
We develop MISO, a multimodal learning framework for fine-scale permafrost and soil taxonomy mapping. It brings together geospatial foundation models, implicit image functions, and contrastive learning to make predictions from sparse observations at arbitrary locations.

## [ICDAR ’25] LIGHT: Multimodal Text Linking on Historical Maps
image: /research/maps.jpg
links: Paper|#, GitHub|#, arXiv|#
LIGHT infers semantic links among words on historical maps. It models polygon geometry, visual features, and language embeddings to reconstruct multi-word place names and unlock geographic knowledge from scanned collections.

## Spatially Varying Dynamics for Spatiotemporal Prediction
image: /research/dynamics.jpg
links: Paper|#, Code|#
We build physics-guided neural networks that learn spatially varying parameters in dynamic systems, capturing local heterogeneity and producing reliable forecasts even when observations are limited.

## Geospatial Knowledge Extraction
image: /research/knowledge.jpg
