# What we are doing

## Spatial Prediction
publications: spatial
animation: spatial-prediction
image: /research/spatial-prediction-cartoon-v2.png
alt: Cartoon showing three field measurement stations on dry land and environmental feature maps feeding a neural network to predict fine-scale spatial variation across the landscape

We develop machine learning methods for fine-scale spatial prediction from sparse observations and multimodal data. Applications include soil and permafrost mapping in Alaska with MISO, and air quality mapping with DeepLATTE.

## Spatiotemporal Modeling
publications: spatiotemporal
image: /research/svpnet-spatiotemporal-forecasting.gif
alt: SVPNet animation of spatially varying physical dynamics
width: 703
height: 703
We model spatial heterogeneity and temporal dynamics for forecasting, anomaly detection, and time-series analysis. Our work includes physics-guided models such as SVPNet, air quality forecasting, and learning relationships between time series and temporal events.

## Historical Map Understanding
publications: maps
image: /research/light-historical-map-understanding.jpg
alt: Historical map with multi-word place names highlighted by LIGHT
width: 574
height: 575
We develop methods to detect, recognize, and link text on historical maps, and to georeference scanned maps. PALETTE, LIGHT, HILL, and the mapKurator pipeline turn map text into structured geographic information.
