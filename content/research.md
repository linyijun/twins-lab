# What we are doing

## Spatial Prediction
animation: spatial-prediction
image: /research/spatial-prediction-cartoon-v2.png
alt: Cartoon showing three field measurement stations on dry land and environmental feature maps feeding a neural network to predict fine-scale spatial variation across the landscape
width: 1254
height: 1254
We develop machine learning methods for fine-scale spatial prediction from sparse observations and multimodal data.

Applications include soil and permafrost mapping in Alaska [[Lin et al., SIGSPATIAL ’26](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1fpe_hijIEL4CEwhRPTm6h8FvzixoQIUy/view?usp=drivesdk); [Lin & Chen et al., SIGSPATIAL ’25](https://doi.org/10.1145/3748636.3764170)] and air quality prediction [[Lin et al., SIGSPATIAL ’17](https://doi.org/10.1145/3139958.3140013); [Lin et al., ICDM ’20](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDM50108.2020.00044)].

We model spatial heterogeneity and temporal dynamics for forecasting [[Lin et al., SIGSPATIAL ’18](https://doi.org/10.1145/3274895.3274907); [Lin & Chiang, SIGSPATIAL ’23](https://doi.org/10.1145/3589132.3625648)], anomaly detection [[Lin & Chiang, BigData ’22](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10020157)], and time-series analysis [[Hu et al., KDD ’26](https://doi.org/10.1145/3770855.3817693)].

## Historical Map Understanding
image: /research/light-historical-map-understanding.jpg
alt: Historical map with multi-word place names highlighted by LIGHT
width: 574
height: 575
We develop methods to detect, recognize, and link text on scanned historical maps [[Lin & Chiang, KDD ’24](https://doi.org/10.1145/3637528.3671589); [Lin et al., ICDAR ’25](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-04617-8_4); [Hao & Lin, GeoSearch ’26](https://geosearch-workshop.github.io/geosearch2026/)]. We develop the mapKurator system to turn map text into structured geographic information [[Kim et al., SIGSPATIAL ’23](https://doi.org/10.1145/3589132.3625579)]. We also georeference historical maps [[Li et al., SIGSPATIAL ’26](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zJ5nt7b9W0n4nZWrqo2yAvOCHmmtDzws/view?usp=drivesdk)] and study spatial reasoning on maps [[Pyo et al., ICLR ’26](https://openreview.net/forum?id=QQCadccQqU)].

We also organize the ICDAR MapText competitions [[Li et al., ICDAR ’24](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-70552-6_22); [Lin et al., ICDAR ’25](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-04630-7_33)].
