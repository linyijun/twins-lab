# Research Projects

## Spatial prediction
image: /research/miso-spatial-prediction.jpg
alt: MISO soil and permafrost mapping in Alaska with observation sites and a detailed inset
width: 809
height: 807
We develop machine learning methods for fine-scale spatial prediction from sparse observations and multimodal data. Applications include soil and permafrost mapping in Alaska with MISO, and air quality mapping with DeepLATTE.

## Spatiotemporal forecasting
image: /research/svpnet-spatiotemporal-forecasting.gif
alt: SVPNet animation of spatially varying physical dynamics
width: 703
height: 703
We study spatiotemporal forecasting by learning spatial heterogeneity and temporal dynamics. Our work includes SVPNet, a physics-guided neural network that learns spatially varying parameters, and graph-based models for air quality forecasting.

## Historical map understanding
image: /research/light-historical-map-understanding.jpg
alt: Historical map with multi-word place names highlighted by LIGHT
width: 574
height: 575
We develop methods to detect, recognize, and link text on historical maps. LIGHT reconstructs multi-word place names, while PALETTE and the mapKurator pipeline support text extraction and geographic search across large map collections.
