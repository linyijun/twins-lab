# Join TWINS Lab

We are building an interdisciplinary group of people excited about machine learning, data science, and spatial thinking. We welcome applicants from diverse backgrounds and value curiosity, kindness, initiative, and careful scholarship.

## Ph.D. positions
We are actively recruiting motivated Ph.D. students in computer science. Strong applicants will have experience in Python and machine learning, and an interest in spatial data, environmental applications, remote sensing, or multimodal AI. Funding is available for selected students.

## Master’s research positions
SIU master’s students interested in research experience may contact us with a short introduction, résumé, and a description of the topics that excite them. Project availability varies by semester.

## Undergraduate research
Undergraduate students are welcome to explore directed research, independent study, and longer-term project opportunities. Prior research experience is not required; persistence and willingness to learn matter most.

## How to apply
Email **yijunlinnn@gmail.com** with the subject “TWINS Lab Application.” Include your CV, unofficial transcript, a short description of your research interests, and—if applicable—links to code, papers, or other work.
