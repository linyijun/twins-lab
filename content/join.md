# Join TWINS Lab

We welcome students from diverse backgrounds who are curious about machine learning and spatial science.

## Ph.D. positions
We are actively recruiting motivated Ph.D. students in computer science. Strong applicants will have experience in Python and machine learning, and an interest in spatial data, environmental applications, remote sensing, or multimodal AI. Funding is available for selected students.

## Master’s, undergraduate, and visiting research
Master’s and undergraduate students, and visiting scholars are welcome to explore research opportunities.
